## Quick takeaways for a website
1. **One-price simplicity is the story** — nearly everything is €1.50. Lean into "cheap, fast, no-fuss toast."
2. **Neon/graffiti aesthetic** — black base, yellow-green lettering, red + pink neon accents, blue awning. Strong dark-mode fit.
3. **The `#SA` is the brand hook** — usable as a hashtag / social handle motif.
4. **"Not just toast!"** is the ready-made hero tagline.
5. Small, punchy menu = easy single-page site (Toasts + Drinks, done).
